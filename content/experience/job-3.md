## Leading Enterprise Solutions Across Africa

As Solution Architect and Solution Delivery Lead at Linx AS, I design and deliver end-to-end enterprise solutions for clients across Southern and Eastern Africa — spanning retail POS, SAP biometric security, government systems, and financial integrations.

### Key Responsibilities

- Design end-to-end solutions aligned with business objectives, leveraging architectural best practices
- Gather and analyse requirements from stakeholders — from C-level executives to end users
- Develop scalable, robust, and cost-effective architectures for software solutions
- Provide technical leadership and guidance to development teams throughout the solution delivery lifecycle
- Identify and mitigate technical risks, proactively addressing potential issues to ensure successful outcomes
- Collaborate with project managers and stakeholders on delivery plans, timelines, and resource allocation
- Run Daily SCRUMs as Scrum Master and manage development sprints
- Ensure all development is merged via Pull Requests to master branches

### Notable Projects

- **Cashbuild POS** (2011–Present): SAP-integrated POS solution with SD, Retail, and FI functionality
- **PnL POS** (2020–Present): SAP-integrated POS with eCommerce, card payments, and Android mobile POS
- **Malawi IFMIS** (2019–Present): SAP implementation for government integrated financial management
- **Delta Brewery POS** (2023–Present): SAP SD-integrated POS for brewery operations
- **Dept of Labour: Compensation Fund** (2024–Present): Biolock biometric solution for high-risk transactions
- **Central Bank of Eswatini** (2019–Present): SAP biometric login protection for critical users
- **ANGLO Kiosk Solution** (2017–2020): Biometric kiosk for Anglo Amplats & Anglo Khumba Iron Ore
