I've shipped production apps in both React and Vue across dozens of projects. Here's my unfiltered take.

## The Short Answer

**Use React when:**
- You need the widest ecosystem and hiring pool
- Your team already knows it
- You're building a large, complex SPA

**Use Vue when:**
- You want a gentler learning curve
- You're a smaller team that values convention
- You want a more batteries-included experience

## Performance

Both are excellent in 2024. The difference is negligible for most applications. Stop worrying about benchmarks and ship your product.

## My Verdict

For most new projects, I reach for React — not because it's better, but because the ecosystem is unmatched. But Vue 3 with the Composition API has closed the gap significantly.

The best framework is the one your team knows well.
